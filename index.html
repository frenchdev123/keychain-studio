Questions

How long does it take?

About 5–7 days, because resin needs time to cure. Ask in DM if you need it sooner.

How do I pay?

Anna confirms the price in DM and tells you how to pay.

Will it look exactly like the preview?

Very close, but each piece is handmade, so colours, ink and glitter flow a little differently every time.

How do I care for it?

Keep it out of long sun and hot cars, clean it with a soft damp cloth, and don't use hand sanitizer on the resin.

Can I order something that isn't on the site?

Yes. Use Send an idea, or message us on Instagram.
